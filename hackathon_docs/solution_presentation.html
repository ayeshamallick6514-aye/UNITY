Pillar 1: Concept Evaluation

The Problem & The Vision

PS-5 Alignment
⚠️ The Problem in Madhya Pradesh
  • Departmental Silos: PWD, Municipal Corporations, MP Jal Nigam, and Discoms plan works independently without shared excavation schedules.
  • Infrastructure Destruction: Freshly surfaced roads are repeatedly cut open for underground pipelines and power conduits.
  • Unstructured Grievance Influx: Helplines receive duplicate, blurry, or unverified complaint images, overloading field engineers with false leads.
🎯 The Project UNITY Concept
  • Unified Nodal Layer: Consolidates civil works timelines across departments onto a single geospatial map.
  • Proactive Spatial Buffering: Algorithms detect scheduling overlaps before permits are issued, enforcing joint execution windows.
  • Intelligent Citizen Grievances: On-device AI (Tesseract.js OCR) and live-camera GPS watermarking guarantee genuine, geo-located citizen submissions.
Target Beneficiaries: District Collectors, Municipal Commissioners, Chief Engineers & All Citizens of MP Concept Score: Direct Problem-Solution Fit

Proactive Infrastructure Conflict Logic:

When Agency A (e.g., MP Jal Nigam) registers an excavation project, the geospatial engine calculates a geometric buffer around the route. If Agency B (e.g., PWD) has a scheduled road-surfacing project overlapping in that zone within a concurrent timeline window, an automatic Inter-Departmental Conflict Block is triggered, requiring joint sign-off before digging authorization.

Algorithm: Coordinate Bounding-Box Proximity + Scheduled Date Collision Matching
Pillar 3: Innovation Highlights

What Makes UNITY Truly Novel?

Key Differentiators
⚡

1. Zero-Cloud-Cost On-Device AI

Instead of routing citizen photos to expensive third-party Cloud Vision APIs (which charge per query and pose data sovereignty concerns), UNITY embeds Tesseract.js directly on the edge. This provides free, instant, and privacy-preserving text extraction for municipal evidence.

🛡️

2. Anti-Fraud Live Evidence Mode

Eliminates internet-downloaded or fabricated grievance submissions by enforcing HTML5 WebRTC live camera streams with device GPS overlays and timestamps, preventing stale or forged complaints from polluting government queues.

🗺️

3. Pre-Excavation Spatial Shield

Shifts the entire paradigm from reactive repair to proactive prevention. Civil departments are prevented from damaging newly paved corridors by enforcing mandatory synchronized utility digging windows.

🔗

4. Universal Multi-Domain Architecture

A single, standardized 4-stage tracking engine (Filed → Assigned → Under Review → Resolved) serving public health, agriculture, urban works, electricity, and rural development under one nodal framework.

Designed for operational sustainability, public data security, and long-term state adoption.
